# NRG VALORANT: competitive path over the last two years

Research cutoff: 7 October 2026. Scope: October 2024–October 2026, with the two full competitive seasons of 2025 and 2026 as the focus. Shanghai remains in progress. Scores are maps won in a match; event records count matches, including any best-of-one matches. EWC and offseason events are distinguished from the VCT qualification circuit.

## Late 2024: a promising preseason

NRG entered the following season with FNS, s0m, Ethan, mada and Verno, coached by bonkar. The October rebuild brought in mada from Moist x Shopify and Verno and bonkar from Oxygen. It combined established veterans with players and a coach coming from North American Challengers. [Contemporary roster report](https://www.vlr.gg/412487/nrg-completes-roster-signs-mada-verno-and-bonkar). Before the VCT season, they won two offseason events:

- **SEN City Classic:** champions, beating Sentinels **2–0** in the final. [Event bracket](https://www.vlr.gg/event/2248/sen-city-classic).
- **SOOP VALORANT League:** champions, beating Gen.G **3–0** in the final. [Event bracket](https://www.vlr.gg/event/2225/soop-valorant-league-2024).

Those wins provided a strong preseason backdrop, but did not themselves qualify NRG for a VCT international event.

## 2025: early exits, a rebuild, then the world title

### January–February: Kickoff disappointment

**Americas Kickoff: 7th–8th, 1–2 match record.** NRG lost **0–2 to Cloud9**, beat **100 Thieves 2–1**, then lost **1–2 to KRÜ**. They missed Masters Bangkok. [Kickoff results](https://www.vlr.gg/event/2274/vct-2025-americas-kickoff/main-event).

In February, **brawk replaced Verno** in the starting roster and **Mikes replaced bonkar** as coach; Verno and bonkar were benched. [Roster announcement coverage](https://www.vlr.gg/450165/nrg-adds-brawk-and-mikes-benches-verno-and-bonkar).

The change was more substantial than the placement suggested. Management said the team had failed in cohesion despite its offseason trophies: the wins had delayed the impact of problems within the group. That is management's account of the situation, not evidence that one individual caused it. brawk arrived from the Moist Challengers program and reunited with former teammate mada, while Mikes took over the coaching position. [Management's explanation and recruitment context](https://www.vlr.gg/450165/nrg-adds-brawk-and-mikes-benches-verno-and-bonkar).

### March–April: another missed Masters

**Americas Stage 1: 7th–8th, 2–4 overall.** A **2–3 group record** got NRG into playoffs, but **100 Thieves eliminated them 2–0** in their first playoff match. NRG missed Masters Toronto as well. [Stage 1 results](https://www.vlr.gg/event/2347/vct-2025-americas-stage-1).

### May: the championship lineup takes shape

Following FNS's retirement, **skuba joined and bonkar returned**. The resulting five were **Ethan, s0m, mada, brawk and skuba**. This is the lineup that later won Champions. [May roster changes](https://www.vlr.gg/486413/nrg-adds-skuba-welcomes-back-bonkar-for-stage-2).

FNS described accumulated disappointment and the recent failures as the tipping point. He said he had reached a point where he no longer wanted to play and did not want another teammate cut to fix the roster. His departure therefore changed both the lineup and its leadership structure; Ethan would lead the eventual title-winning team. This should not be reduced to the claim that removing FNS alone made NRG successful. [FNS's retirement statement, reported contemporaneously](https://www.vlr.gg/486399/fns-retires).

Later interviews explain how the new group developed. bonkar said he sensed its potential within the first five days of practice before the EWC qualifiers, emphasizing chemistry. mada described repeated changes in philosophy and his own shift toward entering and creating space for teammates. He credited daily practice of fundamentals and protocols with the late-season improvement. Those are the participants' explanations, rather than proof of a single decisive cause. [bonkar interview](https://www.hotspawn.com/valorant/news/bonkar-interview-valorant-champions), [mada interview](https://www.sheepesports.com/en/articles/nrg-mada-for-me-it-s-just-the-first-step-i-don-t-want-one-title-i-want-a-legacy/en).

NRG also qualified for the **2025 Esports World Cup** through the Americas qualifier: after losing to 100 Thieves, they won successive elimination matches against 2Game, KRÜ, Cloud9 and MIBR, then beat **100 Thieves 3–1** in the decisive qualification match. Their qualifier record was **6–1**, including the earlier win over LEVIATÁN. [NRG match archive](https://www.vlr.gg/team/matches/1034/nrg/?page=2).

### July: a first international test for the rebuilt team

**Esports World Cup: 5th–8th, 2–1 match record.** NRG beat Team Heretics **1–0** in the opening best-of-one and RRQ **2–0**, before losing **0–2 to Gen.G** in the quarterfinals. EWC was a separate competition, rather than a route into Masters or Champions. [EWC 2025 results](https://www.vlr.gg/event/2449/esports-world-cup-2025).

### July–August: the path to Paris

**Americas Stage 2: runners-up, 7–3 overall.** NRG went **4–1 in groups**, then lost **1–2 to G2** in the upper bracket. They recovered through the lower bracket with wins over **LEVIATÁN 2–0**, **Cloud9 2–1** and **Sentinels 3–1**. Their Champions berth was secured with the Cloud9 victory. G2 then swept NRG **3–0** in the regional grand final. [Stage 2 bracket](https://www.vlr.gg/event/2501/vct-2025-americas-stage-2), [qualification report](https://www.vlr.gg/540406/nrg-books-final-ticket-to-paris-leaf-returns-to-g2-with-win-over-sentinels/).

### September–October: Champions Paris winners

Their group-stage wins were less straightforward than an undefeated record suggests. Against defending champions EDG, NRG fell **11–2 behind on Abyss** before winning **17–15** in overtime, then closed Corrode **13–11**. Against DRX, they lost Haven **4–13**, recovered Corrode **13–11**, then overcame a **0–7 start on Lotus** to win another **13–11**. bonkar said a more proactive approach helped break DRX's initial streak. NRG's ability to recover under pressure was already visible before the playoff run. [EDG match report](https://www.vlr.gg/post/5445994/nrg-sweeps-edg-after-completing-record-tying-comeback), [DRX match report](https://www.vlr.gg/550802/nrg-outlasts-drx-to-advance-to-playoffs).

**Champions Paris: champions, 6–0 matches and 13–4 maps.** Their complete route was:

1. EDward Gaming — **2–0**, group opener.
2. DRX — **2–1**, group qualification match.
3. GIANTX — **2–0**, upper quarterfinal.
4. MIBR — **2–1**, upper semifinal.
5. FNATIC — **2–0**, upper final.
6. FNATIC — **3–2**, grand final on **5 October 2025**.

NRG won their first two grand-final maps, lost the next two, then won the deciding **Sunset**. Their Champions run was undefeated in matches and stayed in the upper bracket throughout. [Champions Paris bracket and results](https://www.vlr.gg/event/2283/valorant-champions-2025).

The final was the defining crisis of the season. **Corrode 13–3** and **Lotus 13–6** gave NRG a commanding start. On Abyss they led **11–1 at halftime**, only for FNATIC to force overtime and win **15–13**. FNATIC then won **Ascent 13–8**, turning an apparent sweep into a fifth map. NRG recovered to win **Sunset 13–5**. The title also made Ethan a two-time Champions winner, and brawk received the event's MVP award. [Detailed grand-final report](https://www.vlr.gg/566226/nrg-holds-off-fnatic-reverse-sweep-wins-champions-paris).

NRG's own account credits a speech from s0m, supported by bonkar, with helping the team reset before Sunset. The organization also reported that only **3.9% of Pick'Em participants** had selected NRG to win; that figure describes those predictions, not an objective probability of victory. [NRG's championship retrospective](https://nrg.gg/blogs/news/valorant-world-champions).

### October–November: a change after the title

s0m stepped back from competition and **keiko joined**. Ethan remained the in-game leader alongside mada, brawk and skuba. The new lineup finished **second at Red Bull Home Ground 2025**, losing the final **0–3 to G2**. [NRG's official roster update](https://nrg.gg/blogs/news/and-we-re-back), [Home Ground results](https://www.vlr.gg/event/2602/red-bull-home-ground-2025).

The roster change also affected the team's personality. In a later interview, skuba described s0m as a vocal emotional leader and keiko as quieter, with considerable firepower. He said skuba and mada took on more responsibility for keeping spirits up. NRG approached the change as building a new team rather than assuming the championship chemistry would carry over automatically. [skuba on adapting after s0m's departure](https://www.thespike.gg/valorant/news/vct-masters-london-nrg-skuba-interview-group-stage/8194).

## 2026: consistent qualification, mixed international results

The starting five have been **Ethan, mada, brawk, skuba and keiko**, with bonkar coaching. [NRG roster update](https://nrg.gg/blogs/news/and-we-re-back).

### January–February: securing Santiago

**Americas Kickoff: third, 4–2 overall.** The 2026 event used triple elimination. NRG lost to MIBR and G2, but survived through the lower bracket. In the deciding match they **reverse-swept MIBR 3–2** after trailing 0–2, earning the third Americas spot at Masters Santiago. [Kickoff bracket](https://www.vlr.gg/event/2682/vct-2026-americas-kickoff).

The qualification match was another close escape. MIBR won Bind **13–9** and Corrode **13–5**, then led **9–3 on Split**, putting NRG on the brink of missing Santiago. NRG reversed that map **13–10**, then dominated Abyss **13–4** and Haven **13–5**. They conceded only nine rounds across the final two maps. The opposition included Verno, formerly part of NRG's 2025 starting five. [Kickoff qualification match report](https://www.vlr.gg/625500/nrg-due-for-santiago-after-dominant-reverse-sweep-over-mibr).

### March: Masters Santiago podium

**Masters Santiago: third, 4–3 overall.** NRG advanced from Swiss at **2–1**, beating XLG and Team Liquid while losing to Paper Rex. In playoffs they beat **BBL 2–1** and **Paper Rex 2–0**, then lost **0–2 to Nongshim RedForce** in the upper final and **1–3 to Paper Rex** in the lower final. [Santiago results](https://www.vlr.gg/event/2760/valorant-masters-santiago-2026).

### April–May: another lower-bracket qualification

There were changes around the players, too. Assistant coach **Strong departed by mutual agreement in March**, after having worked through the 2025 championship season. NRG added **mitch in April**, reuniting him with bonkar and skuba from their Oxygen days. The addition came while NRG were **0–2 in Stage 1 groups**; the later recovery followed it, but that timing alone does not establish causation. [Strong departure](https://www.vlr.gg/647249/nrg-loses-some-strength-as-assistant-coach-strong-departs), [mitch appointment](https://www.vlr.gg/662961/nrg-adds-mitch-as-an-assistant-coach).

**Americas Stage 1: third, 6–3 overall.** After starting groups **0–2**, NRG won their remaining three matches for a **3–2 group record**. They then beat **FURIA, KRÜ and 100 Thieves, each 2–0**, in the lower bracket, securing London qualification. A **2–3 loss to G2** in the lower final ended the regional run. [Stage 1 bracket](https://www.vlr.gg/event/2860/vct-2026-americas-stage-1).

NRG separately qualified for **EWC 2026**, recovering from a **0–2 loss to LOUD** to beat FURIA and LEVIATÁN, then **LOUD 3–0**. They finished third in the qualifier with a **3–1 record**. [NRG match archive](https://www.vlr.gg/team/matches/1034/nrg/).

### June: an early exit in London

**Masters London: 9th–10th, 1–2 in Swiss.** NRG beat **XLG 2–0**, then lost **1–2 to LEVIATÁN** and **1–2 to FUT**. They did not reach playoffs. [London results](https://www.vlr.gg/event/2765/valorant-masters-london-2026).

London had personal significance: skuba said the team wanted to win the event for British teammate keiko. The opener supported that hope, with keiko recording **60 kills across two maps** against XLG. But the run ended against FUT despite very narrow margins: NRG won Lotus **13–11**, lost Haven **12–14**, then lost Fracture **11–13** after nearly recovering from **5–12**. The placement was an early exit, but the elimination match was not a one-sided defeat. [London ambitions and opener interview](https://www.thespike.gg/valorant/news/vct-masters-london-nrg-skuba-interview-group-stage/8194), [FUT elimination report](https://www.vlr.gg/694356/fut-emerges-victorious-in-2-1-nailbiter-over-nrg).

### July: EWC runners-up

**Esports World Cup: second, 4–2 overall.** NRG beat Paper Rex, lost to Vitality, then beat Karmine Corp to leave their group. Playoff wins over **Gentle Mates 2–1** and **BBL 2–1** took them to the final, where **100 Thieves won 3–1**. [EWC 2026 bracket](https://www.vlr.gg/event/2952/esports-world-cup-2026).

After the Gentle Mates quarterfinal, Ethan said NRG had been behind the meta in London and had used a bootcamp to adapt. He described learning Neon with help from mada and keiko, alongside a deeper shared collection of protocols and small coordinated plays. He also said the team could use the energy of an opposing crowd, an echo of the Paris final. This is a useful account of their preparation between London and EWC, not evidence that the meta was the only reason they lost in London. [Ethan's EWC interview](https://insider-gaming.com/nrg-ethan-interview-ewc-2026/).

In July, keiko identified **map-pool depth** as a reason NRG were finding it difficult to turn deep runs into trophies, with frequent map and meta changes complicating preparation. The interviewer's question mistakenly calls their Santiago result second; the verified result was **third**. [keiko's assessment of the team's limitations](https://www.hotspawn.com/valorant/news/nrg-keiko-our-map-pool-isnt-as-strong-as-wed-like-vct-americas-stage-2-interview).

### July–September: unbeaten groups, third in Americas

**Americas Stage 2: third, 6–2 overall.** NRG went **5–0 in groups**, beating MIBR, FURIA, LEVIATÁN, ENVY and Evil Geniuses. In playoffs they beat **LOUD 2–1**, then lost **0–2 to 100 Thieves** and **2–3 to LOUD**. [Stage 2 results](https://www.vlr.gg/event/2977/vct-2026-americas-stage-2).

NRG qualified for **Champions Shanghai through Championship Points**, rather than one of Stage 2's two finalist slots. Their accumulated season results had secured qualification before the final placement was decided. [Qualification scenarios](https://www.vlr.gg/748524/g2-books-tickets-to-shanghai-loud-continues-playoffs-run/), [final qualification report](https://www.vlr.gg/752130/loud-continues-miracle-run-to-qualify-for-champions).

### September–October: defending the title in Shanghai

Their Shanghai opener carried unfinished business: **Nongshim had beaten them in Santiago's upper final**. This time NRG won Lotus **13–5** and Split **13–10**. Split was particularly notable because NRG had largely avoided it since April. bonkar explained that they had moved toward comfort picks, with keiko strongly advocating the composition; mada played Neon and keiko Raze. The result provided evidence of new preparation on a previously neglected map, rather than merely another sweep in the record. [Nongshim rematch and post-match comments](https://www.vlr.gg/759663/nrg-gets-its-revenge-sweeps-nongshim-redforce).

As of **7 October**, NRG are **3–0 in matches and 6–1 in maps**:

- **25 September:** Nongshim RedForce **2–0**.
- **29 September:** Karmine Corp **2–1**, securing playoffs.
- **7 October:** T1 **2–0**, upper quarterfinal.

They are scheduled to face **LOUD in the upper semifinal on 10 October**. Their final Champions placement remains undecided. [NRG current match results](https://www.vlr.gg/team/matches/1034/nrg/), [Shanghai playoff bracket](https://www.vlr.gg/event/2766/valorant-champions-2026).

## Assessment of the competitive arc

**2025 was a turnaround season.** NRG missed both Masters events and changed personnel twice before becoming Americas runners-up and world champions. The May roster and coaching changes preceded the improvement; this sequence alone does not establish which change caused it.

**2026 has been a more consistent qualification season.** NRG finished third at all three Americas VCT events and qualified for Santiago, London and Shanghai. International outcomes have varied: third in Santiago, a Swiss exit in London, and second at EWC, with Shanghai still unfinished.

**G2 and 100 Thieves recur at decisive moments.** G2 stopped NRG in the 2025 Stage 2 final, the 2025 Home Ground final and the 2026 Stage 1 lower final. 100 Thieves eliminated NRG from 2025 Stage 1 and beat them in the 2026 EWC final and Stage 2 upper final. These are useful recurring opponents for telling the team's story, rather than evidence that either matchup is unwinnable.

**The two Champions routes differ.** Paris followed a Stage 2 lower-bracket recovery, but the title itself came through an unbeaten upper-bracket run. Shanghai qualification was supported by results accumulated throughout 2026.
