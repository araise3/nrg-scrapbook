Trivia

[edit]
  • First British organization to reach 1 million USD in earnings.
  • Them and Paper Rex share the title of second most earning organization of 2023 (Total Winnings: $645,000). [124]
  • As of June 2023, they are the first team to win 2 VCT international events in a row, winning LOCK//IN São Paulo and Masters Tokyo.
  • They are the only team to qualify for every Champions tournament since its inception.
  • First organization to reach 2 million USD in earnings.
