KRÜ Esports is an Argentine organization focused on the esports and content creation scenes with the goal of bringing this world to a wider audience with the support of Manchester City, Atlético de Madrid, Barcelona and Argentina striker Sergio Agüero as founder and helping with the connection between the traditional and virtual sports scene.

Trivia

[edit]
  • They are the only team to have played at every international VALORANT tournament in 2021 and 2022.
  • In 2023, despite going winless in the Regular Season of VCT Americas 2023, KRU Esports managed to qualify for Champions 2023 by winning the Last Chance Qualifier without losing a series.
