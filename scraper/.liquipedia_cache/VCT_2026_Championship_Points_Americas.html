Tiebreaker Process

[edit]

Tiebreakers involving 2 teams:

  1. Kickoff, Stage 1 Group Stage & Playoffs, Stage 2 Group Stage & Playoffs, and any 2026 VCT Masters head-to-head Match record
  2. Kickoff, Stage 1 Group Stage & Playoffs, Stage 2 Group Stage & Playoffs, and any 2026 VCT Masters head-to-head Map differential
  3. Kickoff, Stage 1 Group Stage & Playoffs, Stage 2 Group Stage & Playoffs, and any 2026 VCT Masters head-to-head Round differential
  4. Overall VCT Americas regular season standings at the end of Stage 2
  5. A Bo1 Match is held

Tiebreakers involving 3 or more teams:

  1. Stage 1 Group Stage & Stage 2 Group Stage head-to-head Match record
  2. Stage 1 Group Stage & Stage 2 Group Stage Map differential
  3. Stage 1 Group Stage & Stage 2 Group Stage Round differential
  4. Overall VCT Americas regular season standings at the end of Stage 2
  5. A Bo1 Match is held
