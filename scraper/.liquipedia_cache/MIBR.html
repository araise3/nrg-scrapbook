MIBR (also known as Made in Brazil) is a Brazilian esports organization based in São Paulo.

On October 24, 2025, MIBR was acquired by Brazilian tech media company Spun Mídia, MIBR and LOS will sit under the newly created esports division, EZOR. [1]
