ZETA DIVISION (formerly known as JUPITER.) is a Japanese esports organisation. They also feature players and teams in Brawl Stars, Super Smash Bros. Ultimate, Overwatch, Fortnite and PUBG Mobile.

History

[edit]

ZETA DIVISION, formerly known as JUPITER, entered VALORANT in April 2020. They have become one of the most successful teams in Japan since the early days of the game, winning the Japanese Ignition Series and First Strike Japan.

See also: History of ZETA DIVISION
