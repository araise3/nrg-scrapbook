KOI (also known as Movistar KOI for sponsorship purposes) is a Spanish esports organization founded by Ibai and Gerard Piqué in December 2021. The organization entered VALORANT in January 2022, joining the Spanish Regional League. [1]

In January 2024 OverActive Media announced the acquisition of KOI and Movistar Riders, merging it with MAD Lions to form a single organization.

On September 6th, 2025, Riot Games announced that KOI's partnership agreement in the VCT EMEA International League has been terminated, citing a breach of the team's contractual agreement. [2]
