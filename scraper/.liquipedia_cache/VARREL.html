VARREL is a Japanese esports organization based in Yokohama.

On October 15th, 2020, Japanese video game developer/publisher Donuts Co. Ltd. acquired the organization, and renamed the organization from Unsold Stuff Gaming to Donuts Unsold Stuff Gaming. On December 23rd, 2022, the organization rebranded once again, this time it was renamed to VARREL.[1]

On October 15th, 2025, VARREL separated from Donuts Co. Ltd.'s consolidated subsidiaries and became independent under CELLORB's operation.
