Trivia

[edit]
  • Xi Lai is the romanized name of Chinese character 禧来, which means the embodiment of good wishes in Chinese.
  • They are the first and only organization to qualify through a Net Café Tournament and win VCT Ascension, that being CN Nationals S2 - North Division.
