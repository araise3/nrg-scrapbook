Trivia

[edit]
  • Most earning organization of 2024 (Total Winnings: $1,053,872). [51]
  • First Chinese organization to reach 1 million USD in earnings.
  • First Chinese organization to win Champions.
  • Was the first Chinese representative at a Champions event while the game was yet to be approved in the country.
  • In 2023, both EDward Gaming and Chao Hui EDward Gaming almost won all their regional VCT events. Chao Hui EDward Gaming placed second in VCT 2023: GC China Qualifier, but still qualified to VCT 2023: Game Changers East Asia which they also won.
  • EDward Gaming won all 3 China Evolution Series Acts in 2023 (Act 1, Act 2 and Act 3) as well as all 3 VCT China events in 2024. (Kickoff, Stage 1 and Stage 2)
  • They are the first Chinese organization to win an international event, that being VALORANT Champions 2024.
  • During Kickoff 2025, between the Lower Bracket Quarterfinals series against Xi Lai Gaming and the Lower Bracket Semifinals series against Dragon Ranger Gaming, EDward Gaming ammassed a win streak of 32 consecutive rounds, inclusive of 2 13-0 wins on Haven, setting a VCT Record for most number of consecutive rounds won by a team.[52]
