Paper Rex is a Southeast Asian esports organization based in Singapore. It was founded in January 2020 by legendary Counter-Strike player, Harley "dsn" Örwall, together with the founder and chief editor of CSGO2ASIA, Nikhil "nikH" Hathiramani.

Trivia

[edit]
  • First Singaporean organization to reach 1 million USD in earnings.
  • Them and Fnatic share the title of second most earning organization of 2023 (Total Winnings: $645,000). [53]
  • They are the first Southeast-Asian Organisation to reach a Grand Final of an international event, that being (VCT: Stage 2 Masters - Copenhagen)
  • The team is known for their unique style of calling where all players call so there is no In-Game Leader [54]
