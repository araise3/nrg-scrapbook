M80 is an American esports organization based in Boston, Massachusetts.

History

[edit]

Biography

[edit]

M80 entered VALORANT in 2022, signing the core of the ex-Ghost Gaming roster.[1] M80 would go on to compete in Challengers North America; with the organisation qualifying to Ascension in both 2023 and 2024 finishing in second place in both tournaments and thereby missing out on the promotion to the VCT Americas Partnership league twice.

M80 continued their time in Challengers into 2025, finishing Stage 1 in second place. Following the end of the Stage, M80's roster went through some serious changes: assistant coach DrewSpark left to join G2 Gozen, and okeanos and Sato joing Leviatán in VCT Americas.[2][3][4] After these changes, M80 announced that they would be departing Challengers, relinquishing their slot to their players to compete as a free-agent team.[5]

On May 24th 2025, M80 announced the release of their final VALORANT player wedid, ending their time in VALORANT and disbanding the roster.[6]
