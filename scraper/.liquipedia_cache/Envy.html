ENVY (formerly known as Team EnVyUs or Team Envy, often abbreviated to Envy, EnVy or nV) is an American esports organization based in Dallas.

The brand was retired after being acquired by OpTic Gaming in 2022; [1] the organization was later re-established on January 17th, 2025 after Mike "Hastr0" Rufail re-acquired the brand. [2]

Controversy

[edit]

In December 2025, ENVY was criticized for fielding sinatraa as a stand-in for For Those Who Dare 2025, which marked the player's return to competition after last playing in 2021 with Sentinels. The player is banned from Riot events and entered an extended period of inactivity after allegedly failing to cooperate with an investigation into allegations of sexual abuse and assault. [34]
