Giants was a Spanish esports organization with teams in Counter-Strike: Global Offensive, League of Legends, Rainbow Six, Rocket League and VALORANT.

On December 14th, 2023, Giants merged with Excel Esports, creating GIANTX.

History

[edit]

Giants first entered VALORANT on June 2020, and has since become one of the most successful Spanish orgs in the game, reaching top finishes in both local and European tournaments.

See also: History of Giants
