Trivia

[edit]
  • First team known for running the Odin B Main wallbang spray on Ascent in a competitive play.
  • First winners of an international event of the VALORANT Champions Tour. (VCT 2021: Stage 2 Masters - Reykjavík) [102]
  • Sentinels and Gen.G Esports were the oldest (average 23.4) roster at Masters Madrid. [103]
  • First team to win 2 VCT Masters events, winning Masters Reykjavík 2021 and Masters Madrid 2024.
